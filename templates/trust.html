06 / Pause, check, then proceed

Protect Your Business Against Impersonation

Unauthorized parties may reproduce company materials or alter contact information to impersonate Deries Garment.

  1. Check the website address independently. Type deriesgarment.com into your browser and check the address bar. Websites can be cloned and QR codes can be copied.
  2. Confirm representative identities. Use the official channels listed here, especially if a new number or email contacts you.
  3. Verify document authenticity. Document IDs can be copied and watermarks can be removed. A registered ID is not proof of exact file authenticity.
  4. Independently confirm payment instructions. Verify any bank account change through a previously known official channel.
  5. Avoid payments to unverified accounts. Pause if a sender pressures you to bypass confirmation.
  6. Report suspicious communications. Contact us directly before continuing the transaction.

These checks help reduce risk; they cannot eliminate fraud. SHA-256 file matching is not a digital signature and depends on the integrity of the official registry.
